# Credits and licenses

## Instrument samples (`samples/*.js`)

These files are the pre-rendered FluidR3 General MIDI soundfont samples from
[midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) (the
`FluidR3_GM` set, generated from
[FluidR3_GM.sfArk](http://www.synthfont.com/SoundFonts/FluidR3_GM.sfArk)).

They are released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/)
and are included here **unmodified**.

To regenerate this folder from the source, run `scripts/download-samples.ps1`.
